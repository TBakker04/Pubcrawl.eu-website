THREE WAYS

Choose what fits you

Book one or combine them: a large group in your own city, in your own colours, works too.

01

Pubgolf for large groups

A regular game is for up to 12 players. For a department or a whole company we set up several courses at once.

  • Several games running at the same time
  • Staggered starts, so no bar gets crowded
  • One result and one winner for the whole company
02

Pubgolf in your city

Is your city not on the list yet? We build a new course: around the office, near the hotel or at the event location.

  • A route along bars that fit the group
  • Start and finish of your choice
  • Challenges and a quiz about the city itself
03

Pubgolf in your brand style

The game in your company colours, with your logo on the scorecard, the scoreboard and the recap.

  • Brand colours and logo throughout the game
  • Your own names for the teams and the holes
  • A recap to share internally
LARGE GROUPS

All together, without crowding the bar

More than 12 players? Then we turn it into a group event. Everyone plays the same course, but not everyone is in the same bar at once. That keeps it fun for you and for the bar.

1Groups of up to 12Each group gets its own game code and plays with 2 to 6 teams.
2Staggered startsThe groups start fifteen to twenty minutes apart. So a whole crowd never walks into a bar at the same time.
3One live leaderboardAll teams from all groups in one ranking, live on every phone and on a big screen at the drinks afterwards.

GOOD TO KNOW

A game, not a drinking contest

18+ onlyEveryone who plays is 18 or older. The lowest score wins, not whoever drinks the most.
Alcohol-free counts the sameAn alcohol-free drink counts just as much in the score as any other drink. Nobody has to drink to win.
Drinks separateWe provide the game. You pay for the drinks in the bars yourselves, however you want to arrange it.

As many as you like. One game is for 2 to 12 players; with more we make it a group event: groups of up to 12 that start one after another, with one live leaderboard for everyone.

That depends on the group size and on what we tailor for you. You get a proposal with a fixed price up front.

An existing city can be arranged quickly. A new city or your own brand style takes more time. So ask as early as you can; we agree on the planning together.

At least one charged phone with internet per team, and comfortable shoes. No app and no account needed.
