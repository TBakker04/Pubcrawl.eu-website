SPECIAL HOLES

Five holes with a story

THE FINAL HOLE Dragon's Hole Legend says the dragon Smok lived under Wawel, until he drank so much water from the Vistula that he burst. The bartender picks one Polish drink with a story, Żubrówka or krupnik for example, and the teams start a bidding round.

TASTINGSzarlotkaAnother team orders you either a szarlotka or apple juice with cinnamon. One player tastes with eyes closed and guesses.
TRUMPETHejnałEvery hour the trumpet of St Mary's sounds and breaks off mid-melody. Each team hums a call and breaks off at the best possible moment.
TONGUE TWISTERChrząszczA bite with the wódka, and then the best-known Polish tongue twister, all together. The other teams give a score.
BRAINS ONQuizFive questions about Kraków: the dragon, the trumpet, the kings and the city's most famous student.
