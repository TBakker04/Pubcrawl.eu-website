Five holes with a story

THE FINAL HOLE Holger Danske Hole Under Kronborg castle, legend has it, sleeps a giant who only wakes when Denmark is in danger. The bartender picks one Danish drink with a story, and the teams enter a bidding round. After that: on to the clubs.

DRINKING LIKE A DANE
FadølDraught beer is called fadøl. You order and pay at the bar, almost everywhere by card or phone.
After the finaleYou're right in the nightlife area. Dance at Chateau Motel, or catch late jazz at La Fontaine. 18+ only; drink water in between.
