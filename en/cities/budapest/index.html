SPECIAL HOLES

Four holes with a story

THE FINAL HOLE Turul hole The turul is the mythical falcon from the origin story of the Hungarians; it stands in bronze on Buda Castle. The bartender picks one Hungarian drink with a story, Unicum or pálinka for example, and the teams start a bidding round.

DRINKING LIKE A BUDAPESTER
FröccsWine with soda water, in every ratio. A kisfröccs is half and half; light, fresh and good for a long night.
Don't clinkIn Hungary you don't clink beer glasses. The story goes back to 1849; with wine or pálinka it's fine.
