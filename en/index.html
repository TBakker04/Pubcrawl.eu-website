HOW IT WORKS

The city is your golf course.

Pubcrawl is pub golf as a full night out: every bar is a hole, your phone is the scorecard.

1. Book your city

You get a game code and a link for your group of 2 to 12 players. No app needed.

2. Play the holes

At every bar your phone reveals the drink, the story and the challenge. The lowest score wins.

3. Stay sharp

Every 45 minutes the casino alarm goes off. Special holes and photo challenges await along the way, right up to the finale.

THE GAME

Not a bar crawl.A game.

A normal bar crawl goes from bar to bar and sees where you end up. Pubcrawl has a course: a hand-picked route past special bars, with a drink, a challenge and a story at every hole.

Just like golf, your score counts: sips, challenges, chance cards and casino rounds shift the standings. At the end of the night one team is on top, with a photo book full of evidence.

A GAME, NOT A DRINKING CONTEST

Play it your way

Pubcrawl is a night out with a game in it. Choose small glasses, order water in between, or play completely alcohol-free: every city has local alternatives. 18+ only.
