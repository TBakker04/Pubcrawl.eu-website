THE GAME

Not a bar crawl. A game.

A normal bar crawl goes from bar to bar and sees where you end up. Pubcrawl has a course: a hand-picked route past special bars, with a drink, a challenge and a story at every hole.

Just like golf, your score counts: sips, challenges, chance cards and casino rounds shift the standings. At the end of the night one team is on top, with a photo book full of evidence.

HOW IT WORKS

Five steps to the finale

01

Pick your city and your night

Book for your group, from 2 to 12 players. You get a game code and a link. No app needed: the game runs in the browser of every phone.

02

Every bar is a hole

The draw makes 2 to 6 teams. At every hole your phone reveals the drink, the story of the place and the challenge. You only learn the route as you go.

03

Casino alarm

Every 45 minutes the alarm goes off on everyone's phone at once. Roulette, blackjack, a horse race or the wheel of fortune: the standings can always flip.

04

Special holes

Every city has its own special holes: a quiz about the city, local drinking rituals and a closing hole from one of the city's legends.

05

Photo bombs and the finale

Photo challenges pop up along the way. At the end comes the finale with the results, prizes and a recap of your best photos, ready for the group chat.
