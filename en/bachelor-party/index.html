WHY PUBGOLF

More than a pub crawl

Everyone joins inIn teams everyone plays along, including the in-law nobody knows and the friend who never drinks. After a few holes the group is one.
The route is readyWe picked the bars: old cafés, local specialities and a final where it stays open late. All you do is walk.
Suspense until the endQuiz questions, photo challenges, chance cards and the casino alarm every 45 minutes. The standings can flip until the last hole.
A keepsakeAfter the final you make a recap with the result and your best photos, plus an Instagram story to share.
TIPS

Make it their night

1Think of team names in advanceTeam names are on the scoreboard all night. A nod to the guest of honour always works.
2Mix the groupMix friends, family and colleagues across the teams. That way everyone gets to know each other.
3Choose Friday or SaturdayThat is when every bar on the route is open. Each city page shows the start times that fit.
4Plan the food around itA good lunch before or a dinner halfway makes the night better. Water between the holes is part of it.
LARGE GROUPS

All together, without crowding the bar

More than 12 players? Then we turn it into a group event. Everyone plays the same course, but not everyone is in the same bar at once. That keeps it fun for you and for the bar.

1Groups of up to 12Each group gets its own game code and plays with 2 to 6 teams.
2Staggered startsThe groups start fifteen to twenty minutes apart. So a whole crowd never walks into a bar at the same time.
3One live leaderboardAll teams from all groups in one ranking, live on every phone and on a big screen at the drinks afterwards.

A GAME, NOT A DRINKING CONTEST

A party for everyone

18+ onlyEveryone in the group is 18 or older and carries valid ID. The lowest score wins, not whoever drinks the most.
Alcohol-free counts the sameAn alcohol-free drink counts just as much in the score as any other drink. Handy for anyone who is pregnant, driving or simply does not drink.
Look out for each otherChoose small glasses and feel free to skip a drink. Bar staff always have the final say.
