HOW IT WORKS · THE RULES

The city is your golf course

Pubgolf turns a pub crawl into a game. Every bar is a hole, your phone is the scorecard and, just like in golf, the lowest score wins. Read below how it works; it takes a few minutes.

BEFORE YOU START

From booking to first hole

1. Pick a city and a dateBook on your city’s page. You pick the day and a start time at which all bars along the way are open.
2. You get a game codeThe code and the link arrive by email right away. One code is enough for the whole group.
3. Share the linkForward the link to your group. The game runs in the browser: no app, no account.
4. Make the teamsOn the day itself the game opens. Split the group into teams, choose team names and walk to hole 1.
THE RULES

How to play a hole

Every hole goes the same way. After a few bars everyone knows the rhythm.

1Walk to the barYour phone shows the way. Is a bar closed or full? Then the game points you to a backup bar a few minutes’ walk away.
2Reveal the drinkThe drink card shows what belongs to this hole, with the story of the bar or the drink.
3Count your scoreEach team counts its sips: that is the score for this hole. Nobody has to drink fast; you play at your own pace.
4Fill in the scoreboardOne person enters the scores. Everyone sees the standings on their own phone right away, and then the next hole opens.
THE SCORE

The lowest score wins

Just like in golf, you want to finish as low as possible. Your score per hole is the start; along the way you can win or lose points.

ALONG THE WAY

What keeps the night exciting

Special holesEvery city has a few holes with a rule of their own: a quiz about the city, a local drinking ritual or a challenge that only works there.
Casino alarmEvery 45 minutes the alarm goes off on all phones at once: wheel of fortune, horse race, blackjack or roulette. You play for points, not for money.
Photo challengesIn between, a photo challenge pops up. The first team with the photo wins a point. All photos go into the photo book.
Chance cards and jokersSometimes a team draws a card that shakes up the standings. A joker you save for the right moment.
The finalAfter the last hole you see the final standings. Choose the player of the night together and find out what awaits the losers.
Recap and photo bookAfterwards you make a recap to share. Download it in time: the photos are deleted automatically after 7 days.
A GAME, NOT A DRINKING CONTEST

Play it your way

18+ onlyEveryone in the group is 18 or older and carries valid ID.
Alcohol-free counts the sameAn alcohol-free drink counts just as much in the score as any other drink. You play for the win like everyone else.
You choose your glassOrder small sizes, skip a drink and have water between the holes. If someone is not doing well, stop.
